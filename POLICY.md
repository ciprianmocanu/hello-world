# Claim Zero — Alpha Operating Policy

Effective: 20 September 2026

Claim Zero is a public experimental service for documenting verifiable discoveries and testing whether buyers will fund clearly scoped discovery hunts. This alpha is intentionally narrow.

## 1. What Claim Zero is

A contributor may submit a falsifiable claim supported by lawful evidence. A buyer may post a discovery-hunt request with objective acceptance criteria and an indicative budget. Public submissions are currently recorded as GitHub issues.

## 2. What Claim Zero is not

Claim Zero is not an investment product, security, broker, employment agency, lottery, gambling product, bounty escrow service, intellectual-property registry, title registry, ownership registry, or guarantee of payment or future income.

A Claim Zero attribution does not create ownership or control over a third-party person, company, product, property, artwork, trademark, patent, copyright, domain, data set, place or idea.

## 3. First proven, not first shouted

A vague or incomplete submission does not reserve a discovery. Priority is based on the earliest complete contribution that is sufficiently evidenced to be independently evaluated. Prior art may invalidate, merge or narrow a claim.

## 4. Challenges and corrections

Public claims may be challenged with evidence. The goal is an accurate record, not preservation of status. Where evidence shows that a claim is not novel, is materially misleading, or infringes these rules, it may be corrected, merged, rejected or removed.

## 5. Lawful evidence only

Do not submit or request:

- private personal data or doxxing;
- medical, financial or similarly sensitive personal records;
- hacked, leaked or unlawfully accessed material;
- trade secrets or confidential business information;
- impersonation, deception or bypassing access controls;
- covert surveillance of private persons;
- trespass or entry into non-public areas;
- unlawful discrimination or targeting;
- dangerous instructions or tasks;
- copyrighted material beyond what you have a lawful basis to quote, link to, describe or upload.

Public sources, links, citations, original observations made lawfully in public, and material submitted by a rights-holder are preferred.

## 6. Buyer hunts

Posting an alpha hunt request is currently free and is not a purchase. A paid pilot may begin only after the buyer receives and accepts, before payment:

- scope and acceptance criteria;
- total price and applicable taxes;
- what deliverable will be supplied;
- delivery method;
- cancellation/refund conditions;
- any contributor compensation rules;
- payment method and seller identity.

No task should be represented as funded until funds have actually been received or validly secured under the agreed payment method.

## 7. Contributor economics

The public alpha does not create rights to royalties, equity, securities, ownership or future income. If Claim Zero later monetises a specific contribution and offers revenue sharing, the applicable economics must be disclosed and accepted before that monetisation begins.

No contributor should be required to pay money for a chance to earn money from a claim.

## 8. Privacy

The alpha deliberately avoids collecting a separate user database. Submissions are made through GitHub and are therefore subject to GitHub's account, privacy and platform terms. Users should treat all issue submissions as public and should not publish information they would not want publicly indexed.

## 9. Intellectual property

Contributors retain whatever rights they lawfully hold in their own original material. By intentionally submitting material to a public Claim Zero issue, they permit Claim Zero to display, quote, link to, evaluate and discuss that submission for operating the alpha. This permission does not transfer ownership of third-party works.

## 10. Accuracy

Claim Zero may use automated tools and human review, but neither acceptance nor publication is a warranty that a claim is true, novel, commercially valuable, non-infringing or complete. Material claims should remain linked to supporting evidence so they can be independently assessed.

## 11. Alpha changes

The service, name, rules and economic model may change or be discontinued. If a future paid transaction occurs, the terms accepted for that transaction control that transaction and should not be retroactively changed.

## Operating directive

**Legal. Moral. Procedural. Evidence before hype. Payment only for explicit value. No fabricated traction, revenue, users or results.**
