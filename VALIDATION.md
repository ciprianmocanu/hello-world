# Claim Zero — Validation Rules

The purpose of the alpha is not to prove the idea is good. It is to find out whether a buyer pays for it.

## Current wedge

Buyer: history creator / podcast / newsletter / publisher  
Offer: 5 source-backed, underexposed true-story leads  
Founding pilot price: €29 fixed  
Founder acquisition spend: €0

## Funnel

Track only real events:

1. Relevant prospect identified from a current public source.
2. Individual outreach sent to an explicitly published contact/editorial/episode-idea channel.
3. Reply received.
4. Prospect provides a real brief or asks substantive commercial questions.
5. Scope and acceptance criteria agreed.
6. Payment actually received from an independent third party.
7. Deliverable accepted (or refund/cancellation rule applied).

## What does not count

- email opens if not independently available without paid tracking;
- social impressions;
- founder self-payments;
- test transactions;
- compliments without purchase intent;
- fake testimonials;
- free work retroactively described as revenue;
- affiliate credits that cannot be withdrawn as money.

## Stage 1 test

Maximum: 20 carefully selected prospects. No bulk scraping or mass spam.

Before sending, each prospect should satisfy at least two:
- currently active;
- produces history / true-story content regularly;
- publicly asks for ideas, pitches or collaboration;
- has evidence of monetisation (ads, membership, sponsors, paid publication, products);
- the €29 deliverable can plausibly save them research time.

## Interpretation after 20 qualified contacts

### A — At least 1 paid pilot
Continue. Deliver exceptionally, learn what the buyer actually valued, then test price and repeatability.

### B — Replies / briefs, but zero payments
Demand may exist but the offer, trust, price or payment friction is wrong. Interview the objections before changing the whole product.

### C — Almost no replies
The positioning or buyer segment is probably weak. Do not solve this with more volume. Change message or segment.

### D — Positive feedback but repeated “we do this ourselves / not worth paying”
Kill the history-research wedge. Preserve the underlying discovery mechanism and test a higher-value buyer problem.

## Hard rule

Do not spend founder money to rescue a weak organic test. Paid ads, domains, subscriptions, APIs, credits and paid hosting require explicit founder approval and must never be used to manufacture apparent traction.

## Revenue truth

Until money from an independent buyer has been received, verified revenue remains **€0.00**.
