# CLAIM ZERO

**Discover it before the world does.**

Claim Zero is a public-alpha experiment in source-backed discovery. The current validation wedge is deliberately narrow: can a creator or publisher pay for a small pack of underexposed, well-sourced true-story leads?

## Founding pilot

**€29 fixed pilot — 5 source-backed story leads** matched to one agreed theme, period, country or editorial angle.

Each lead includes:
- a concise hook;
- why it may be editorially useful;
- credible sources and primary sources where available;
- disputed or uncertain details clearly marked;
- a suggested episode/article/short-form angle.

Payment is never requested before the scope, acceptance criteria, seller identity, deliverable and cancellation/refund terms are explicitly agreed in writing.

See: [`pilot.html`](./pilot.html)

## Free sample

A three-lead sample pack is public here: [`sample-pack.md`](./sample-pack.md)

## Contributor side

Discovery claims are free. A contributor does **not** acquire ownership of a company, creator, product, property, IP or other third-party asset merely by submitting a discovery. The alpha records attribution to useful evidence; economic rights require separate explicit terms before monetisation.

## First Directive

**Legal. Moral. Procedural. Evidence before hype.**

Additional founder rule:

> **Zero founder spend without explicit prior approval.**

No third-party app, API, hosting plan, subscription, ad spend, domain, credit pack or other paid service may be purchased or activated at the founder's expense unless the founder explicitly approves that specific payment first. A saved card, connected account or free trial is not approval.

## Alpha constraints

Do not submit:
- hacked or leaked material;
- confidential business information;
- private personal, medical or financial data;
- trade secrets;
- evidence obtained through trespass, deception, harassment or unlawful surveillance;
- claims presented as fact when the evidence does not support them.

Claim Zero is an experiment, not an investment product, lottery, broker, promise of income or ownership registry for third-party assets.
