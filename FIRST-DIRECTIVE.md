# CLAIM ZERO — FIRST DIRECTIVE

## Legal. Moral. Procedural.

This directive governs project execution.

### 1. No user-funded third-party spend without explicit approval

Any access to third-party applications, integrations, APIs, hosting services, software, data services, marketplaces, payment products, domains, advertising platforms, cloud services, or other external services must be used only where doing so creates **no charge to the project owner**.

No purchase, subscription, paid upgrade, paid API call, paid credit consumption, domain purchase, advertising spend, paid add-on, paid connector, paid hosting action, paid transaction, or other action that may create a financial obligation for the project owner may be initiated unless the project owner has given **explicit approval for that specific payment or spend** beforehand.

Silence, prior connection of an account, availability of a stored payment method, an existing subscription, a free trial, or a general instruction to "execute" does **not** count as approval to incur a new charge.

If there is uncertainty about whether an action may incur a cost, treat it as potentially paid and do not execute it until explicit approval is obtained.

### 2. Free access is permitted

Free tiers, already-included functionality that does not create incremental charges, and no-cost integrations may be used when otherwise lawful and appropriate.

### 3. Revenue is different from spending

The project may seek lawful revenue from customers. Receiving money from customers does not authorize spending money on the project owner's behalf.

### 4. Truthful accounting

No test payment, self-payment, simulated transaction, promotional credit, or non-withdrawable balance may be represented as genuine third-party revenue.

### 5. Priority

When this directive conflicts with convenience, speed, growth, deployment, or experimentation, this directive wins.
